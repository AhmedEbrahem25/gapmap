# Problem statement

## The student

A student receives a grade or a corrected paper but cannot answer these questions:

- Which concept did I get wrong?
- Was the problem understanding the concept, applying it, calculating, or a missing prerequisite?
- Which lecture, and which slide or page, should I review?
- After reviewing, how do I check that I actually understood it?

Without answers, the student either re-reads everything or nothing, and the exam ends the learning
instead of directing it.

## The instructor

The instructor cannot analyse every student's mistakes by hand. A class of 20 students answering
9 questions already produces 180 answers. Patterns such as "most of the class confuses the Scrum
Master with the Product Owner" stay hidden, so re-teaching decisions are guesses.

## Why existing tools don't solve it

- **Grade books and LMS reports** show scores per question or per exam, not per concept, and do not
  point to the course material.
- **General chatbots** only know what the student types. They don't see the real exam answers, don't
  use the instructor's slides as their source, and can invent references.

## Problem to solve

Turn approved exam results into an evidence-based diagnosis per student and per class. Link every
gap to the instructor's own material, and close the loop with targeted practice and a retest,
without changing official grades and without depending on an AI service for the core result.
